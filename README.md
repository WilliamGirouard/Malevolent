# 🌲 Malevolent

> A 2D horror game: the sole survivor of a forest massacre, hide, stay silent, and escape the monster hunting you.

## 📖 Story

A group of friends is gathered around a campfire in a dark forest. A piercing screech echoes through the trees, and within seconds, you are alone. Around you lie the bodies of your friends.

You run into the forest. Something is following you.

## 🎮 Gameplay

Your goal is simple: **survive and escape the monster that killed your friends.**

- **Hide** in suitable spots to break the monster's line of sight.
- **Succeed at QTEs** (Quick Time Events) to avoid making noise. Each one is a random key combination that must be pressed within a very short time.
- **Fail a QTE** and you risk one of two outcomes:
  - the monster hears you and **runs straight at you**, or
  - a **jump scare** hits the screen.

### Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `←` / `→` | Move left / right |
| `↑` | Jump over obstacles |
| Random keys | Quick Time Events (shown on screen) |

## 🏁 Endings

There are two ways to escape:

1. **Reach civilization** safe and sound.
2. **Find the shaman**, who can destroy the monster.
